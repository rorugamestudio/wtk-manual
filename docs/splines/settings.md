# Spline Settings

Spline display options are in the **Settings** tab of the **More** module. They apply wherever splines are edited.

## Spline Display

**Always Show Splines**
:   Draws splines even when you're not editing them.

**Line Thickness** and **Knot Size**
:   How thick the curves are, and how big the knots.

**Show Knot Indexes**
:   Writes each knot's number next to it.

**Use Tangent Mode Knot Shapes**
:   Draws each knot with a shape that tells its type (Linear, Auto or Bezier).

**Draw Occluded** and **Occluded Opacity**
:   Draws splines hidden behind other objects, faded.

**Samples Per Segment**
:   How smoothly curves are drawn.

## Spline Editing

**Edit Selected Containers Only**
:   Only the spline containers you have selected show editable knots. Useful in busy scenes.

## Colors

Colors of the **Spline**, the **Closed Spline Fill**, the **Selected Spline** and its fill, **Knot** and **Selected Knot**, the **Open Spline Endpoint**, the **Knot Rotation Marker**, **Tangent**, **Selected Tangent** and **Tangent Line**, and the **Creation Offer** shown before you click to add a knot.

The Roads, Buildings and Terrain modules have their own **Splines** settings (line color, closed fill and thickness) for the splines they edit.
